name: Keep it simple retake
version: 1
group: starter
style: keep-it-simple
---
The last take wins. When the same line is said more than once, keep the last take, unless that last take is broken or trails off unfinished. Then keep the last complete take instead.

A line that corrects a fact is not a retake. If someone says a number and then says the right number, both lines stay, because the second line fixes the first one and the fix is part of what was said.

A line that is said twice on purpose stays twice. Prayers, refrains, and a point made again in different words for emphasis are not retakes, so leave both sayings in.
