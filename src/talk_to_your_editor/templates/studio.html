Keyboard shortcuts
  • T open or close the timeline
  • R replay 2 seconds around the picked cut
  • [ and ] nudge the picked piece's start and end edges by 10 ms (Shift: 50 ms). Edges snap to word starts and ends; hold Option for a free nudge the other way.
  • Cmd+Shift+S split the piece at the playhead
  • Cmd+Shift+M send the picked words to the editor
  • Cmd+Backspace cut the picked words
  • M pin a note at the playhead
  • Cmd+X / Cmd+C / Cmd+V cut, copy, paste a piece
  • Cmd+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z undo, redo
  • Delete take out the picked piece, no gap left
  • Space play or pause
