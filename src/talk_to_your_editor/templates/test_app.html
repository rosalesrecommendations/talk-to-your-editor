How this works

Drop in a video or a job folder. Watch the edit and read the words next to it. Cut words or bring them back right in the transcript, and pin a comment where something needs to change. When you are ready, Make the edit runs the choices on the card and builds a new edit from your words and your comments.

Times everywhere on this page match the video you are watching: the edited video's clock.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts: Space play or pause · K pause · J back 5 seconds · L forward 5 seconds · ←/→ step 1 second · Shift+←/→ step 5 seconds · M pin a note here · Cmd+Z undo · Cmd+Shift+Z redo.

About direction and captions

What you say here outranks the saved choices for this video. Anything that is not one of the app's choices is saved as overall guidance for the model. Captions come with the first edit, with ums and uhs hidden in the caption text only. The sound and the transcript keep them. You can change that where the captions are handed back, under Revised edit.

The stages, in order

1

Your video on its timeline

The marks are every quarter of the video. No words are written down yet, and nothing has been cut.

2

Write down the words

Transcribe on its own if you want to read the words before any cuts are made. The words then open on the review screen.

3

Run the Remove passes you want

Retakes: when a line is said more than once, one take stays. Word gaps: pauses are shortened. Ums and uhs: filler words come out. Each one is its own row in the Remove panel, and after Transcribe each one is its own button on the review screen.
