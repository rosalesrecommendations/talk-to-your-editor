How this works

Drop in a video or a job folder. Watch the edit and read the words next to it. Cut words or bring them back right in the transcript, and pin a comment where something needs to change. When you are ready, Make the edit runs the choices on the card and builds a new edit from your words and your comments.

Times everywhere on this page match the video you are watching: the edited video's clock.

Bringing in a job

Drop anywhere on this page. No path typing, no Terminal. The app works on the copy it takes in; your original folder is never changed. Several files dropped together are parts of one job, in the order you put them in.

Test videos

A video can be marked as a test video in the intake pop-up or in Settings. The app does not learn from a test video: nothing from it is saved as a rule or a grade.

What an edit costs

The estimate covers writing the words down and the editing passes the choices turn on. Work that runs on this computer costs nothing.

A key used for this session only is kept in memory, never written to a file, never shown back, and gone when the server stops.     Writing down the words…

    Click the first word of the part to change, then the last word, and retype the words exactly as they should read. One word can become two or three, or a few fragments can become one. Changing words changes the words in the transcript only. The sound and the timing never change, and a video you build from the edit still shows the original words in its captions.
