Transcript

The words of the edit, in order. Words that were cut are struck through and dimmed. Click a word to jump the video to it and pick it, then cut the picked words or bring them back with the buttons under the words. Shift and click picks every word from the first picked word to the one clicked. Every change passes the same safety check as a direction change.

Click the first word of the part to fix, then the last word, and retype the words exactly as they should read. One word can become two or three, or a few fragments can become one. Fixing words changes the words in the transcript only. The sound and the timing never change, and a video you build from the edit still shows the original words in its captions.
