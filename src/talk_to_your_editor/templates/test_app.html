Transcript

The words of the edit, in order. Words that were cut are struck through and dimmed. Drag across words to pick a whole phrase, or click a word to pick just that word; the strip under the words cuts the pick or brings it back, and Backspace cuts it too. Clicking struck-through words picks exactly those words, so bringing words back brings back only what you picked. Cmd+Z undoes the last change, and Cmd+Shift+Z does it again. Every change passes the same safety check as a direction change.

Cmd+Z to undo · Drag picks a phrase · Backspace cuts it

Click the first word of the part to fix, then the last word, and retype the words exactly as they should read. One word can become two or three, or a few fragments can become one. Fixing words changes the words in the transcript only. The sound and the timing never change, and a video you build from the edit still shows the original words in its captions.
